# Problem 3 — Advanced components

Some things are not shapes and are not simple interactive controls. A diagram is the clearest case: a flowchart, a graph with nodes and connections, a tree. It is a structure made of many parts that relate to each other, not one object with an outline.

Problem 2 covers editing a made shape back into its parts. But a diagram may be too much for that frame — it is not one shape remembering its faces, it is a whole set of shapes plus the relationships between them, and editing it means editing that structure. So this may be a separate special case, alongside lines and single shapes.

Parked for now. To be worked out later, once we see whether Problem 2 stretches to cover it or whether advanced components need their own treatment.
